What is Midnight Abyss?

Midnight Abyss is a rules‑light survival RPG set in the crushing dark of the deep ocean. Crews risk the trenches for freedom, answers, and air. Oxygen is currency. Pressure is the enemy. Every dive is a wager.

• Rules‑light • Faction play • Vehicle dives • Oxygen economy

Accessibility First

Designed for long reading sessions: mid‑tone pages, strong contrast, open letterforms, and dyslexia‑friendly typesetting.

Status

Core manuscript ~215 pages (pre‑layout). Funding plans and additional resources will be announced here when ready.

Features

Oxygen Economy

Track a shared O₂ pool. Every action has cost, every breath has weight.

Vehicle Dives

Collaborative submersible missions with pooled successes against trench hazards.

Factions in Conflict

Oceanic Union, Abyssal Corps, Pelagic League, Trench Confederacy — pick your allies, count your enemies.

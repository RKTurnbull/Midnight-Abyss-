What is Midnight Abyss?

Midnight Abyss is a rules‑light survival RPG set in the crushing dark of the deep ocean. Crews risk the trenches for freedom, answers, and air. Oxygen is currency. Pressure is the enemy. Every dive is a wager.

• Rules‑light • Faction play • Vehicle dives • Oxygen economy

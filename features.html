Game Features

  • 🎲 Rules-light design focused on tension, oxygen management, and meaningful risk.
  • 🌊 Modular survival mechanics covering pressure, salvage, deep dives, and sub operations.
  • ⚓ Flexible structure for both one-shots and long-form campaign play.
  • 💀 Human stories of endurance, debt, and discovery beneath the sea.
  • 🌀 World of scarcity and awe where O₂, light, and loyalty are the only true currencies.
